# Casa Bellucci — Brand Spec (Sicilian Summer Redesign)

## Mood
Sun-drenched Sicilian summer terrace. Belmond Grand Hotel Timeo meets Dolce & Gabbana Sicily campaigns meets hand-painted Taormina ceramic tile art. Golden hour, lemon groves, bougainvillea on whitewashed walls, the Tyrrhenian Sea in the background.

## Typography

- **Display**: Playfair Display (400/500/600/700/800; italic) — editorial serif for all headings
- **Body**: Cormorant Garamond (300/400/500/600) — luxurious body serif
- **Script**: Allura / Italianno — handwritten brush-script subtitles ("Sizilien in Berlin")
- **Mono/UI**: system mono — for numerics, metadata only

## Posture rules

1. **One decisive accent at a time.** Lemon yellow OR terracotta OR Mediterranean blue per section — never all three competing.
2. **Lemon as signature motif.** Hand-painted SVG lemon branches as section dividers, watercolor lemon accents in About, lemon pattern in Majolika tiles, lemon grove pattern in Footer.
3. **Majolika ceramic tile pattern.** Blue + yellow Sicilian tile motif as background texture in Menu cards.
4. **Warm light, never cold.** Sandstone over grey. Cream over white. Golden hour photography only.
5. **Editorial typography.** Large serif headlines (clamp 48–96px), generous letter-spacing for eyebrows, script for romantic accents.
6. **One flourish per section.** Floating lemon branch in Hero. Parallax in Terrace. Hand-drawn map in Contact. Don't compete.
7. **Dark contrast = warm dark.** Events section uses Med blue or dark terracotta — never charcoal grey.

## Signature visual elements

- Hand-painted SVG lemon branches (stem, leaves, fruits with watercolor texture)
- Majolika tile pattern (blue/yellow ceramic motif)
- Hand-drawn map of Kurfürstendamm location
- Lemon grove pattern in footer (repeated lemon branch motif)
- Script subtitle ("Sizilien in Berlin", "Una Tavola in Sicilia")

## Photography style

- Golden hour, warm color grading
- Long shadows, sun flare welcome
- Mix: food close-ups, terrace atmosphere, interior warmth, hands & wine glasses
- No staged/sterile shots — everything should feel lived-in
