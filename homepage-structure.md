# Casa Bellucci Homepage Structure

## Positioning

Casa Bellucci should feel like a premium Italian all-day restaurant and bar on Kurfürstendamm, not a generic trattoria. The audience is affluent: dinner guests often spend around 80 EUR per person, with an older, quality-sensitive crowd in the evening and younger affluent guests for breakfast, lunch, and summer terrace moments.

The homepage should sell atmosphere, confidence, location, and dining occasions. Detailed SEO content and long-form pages should live on dedicated pages later.

## Homepage Sections

### 1. Hero

Goal: establish brand, location, and premium all-day positioning immediately.

Content direction:
- Casa Bellucci
- Italian restaurant and bar on Kurfürstendamm
- Breakfast, lunch, dinner, terrace
- Primary CTA: reserve
- Secondary CTA: view menus

The video background stays. Hero copy should remain flexible because final SEO wording will be decided later.

### 2. All-Day Concept

Goal: explain that Casa Bellucci is active across the full day, not only dinner.

Three moments:
- Breakfast and brunch
- Lunch and terrace
- Dinner and bar

This section should feel editorial and premium, not like generic feature cards.

### 3. Signature Moments / Menu

Goal: create appetite and signal price level without showing the full menu.

Use curated highlights from breakfast, lunch, dinner, wine, and drinks. Link to menus or future menu pages.

### 4. Sommerterrasse

Goal: make the summer terrace a major conversion and image driver.

Audience:
- Younger affluent guests in the morning and midday
- Evening guests looking for aperitivo and dinner atmosphere

Keep the section visually strong and seasonal.

### 5. Gallery / Atmosphere

Goal: show food, room, terrace, and mood with a small number of strong images.

Avoid a generic image dump. The gallery should feel curated and editorial.

### 6. Reservation

Goal: convert.

Include:
- Reserve CTA
- Phone
- Opening hours
- Short confidence-building copy

Keep it calm and direct.

### 7. Contact / Location

Goal: reinforce Kurfürstendamm as a premium signal and make visiting easy.

Include:
- Kurfürstendamm 63
- Charlottenburg / Berlin
- Map or map link
- Contact details

## Pages That Should Be Separate Later

- Breakfast / Brunch
- Lunch
- Dinner
- Wine / drinks
- Sommerterrasse
- Press
- Reservation
- Contact
- Imprint
- Privacy

## Design Rules

- Keep the homepage focused. Do not add more primary homepage sections without a strong reason.
- Avoid long SEO text blocks on the homepage.
- Avoid over-rustic Sicily cliches. The tone is Kudamm, Italian elegance, summer, service, and quality.
- Avoid too many small cards. Prefer confident editorial sections, strong imagery, generous spacing, and clear CTAs.
- German user-facing text must use real umlauts.
